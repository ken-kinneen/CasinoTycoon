CORRUPTED CASINO OWNER

You are Victor Vane. You own The Lucky Duck, a strip-mall casino wedged between a pawn shop and a bail bondsman. It smells like carpet cleaner and regret.

You have $200, four slot machines, and a dream: a cathedral of neon on the Las Vegas strip. Between here and there are a lot of other people's pockets.

How to be terrible
Walk the floor. WASD or arrows. Drag the mouse to orbit, scroll to zoom, Q spins the camera.
Work. Walk up and press F: the street (ads), the safe (vault crack), the desk (upgrades). Click a table to deal. 1 2 3 jump straight there.
Hoppers. Guests spend into their machine's hopper. It isn't yours until you haul it to the safe. A full hopper stops earning.
Heat. How much the authorities notice you. Inspectors drop by; the envelope costs money. Bribes bring heat down.
Guests. Drunks have wide margins at the table. Sharps spend less and are hard to beat. Whales bet big and love prestige.
The dream. Buy the next casino from the Expansion tab. Skills and ad campaigns follow you. Vegas is the goal.
Arrange. Press G or click Arrange Floor in the sidebar, then click any machine or table — it follows your cursor. Click to place, R to rotate, Esc to cancel. Green = valid, red = blocked.
